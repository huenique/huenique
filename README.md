**About me**

I'm Hju Kneyck Flores, a software engineer in the Philippines. I started in 2019 as an IT student at Manuel S. Enverga University, writing scrapers and small apps for problems around me: a PHLPost delivery notifier, a scraper for Freedom of Information requests, an SMS gateway that ran on an Android phone. While still in school I worked as an operations and project manager at SurPath Hub, where the internal tools I built cut process time by about 30% and taught me to understand how a business runs before writing code for it. At KodeCraft I moved into Web3 and trading infrastructure in Rust and TypeScript, and launched a crypto derivatives dashboard that took execution latency from about 30 seconds to under 10 milliseconds. In 2025 I built data ingestion pipelines with the founding team at Tereo.AI, an AI brand protection startup, then started shipping production AI systems for clients: a TV commercial detection service on Azure, a pipeline that turns meeting transcripts into verified tasks, and multi-agent video analysis. Alongside that I built my own trading desk, which now runs live across 16 exchanges. Today I work as an independent solutions architect for AI systems. Most of my work arrives as an outcome rather than a spec, so I scope the problem with the people who have it, build it end to end, and test it against real data before it ships.

I build crypto trading systems, then try to break them before the market does.
